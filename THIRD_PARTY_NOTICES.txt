VST3 interface definitions

The ABI types, interface identifiers and virtual method layouts in src/abi.rs
are adapted from the official Steinberg VST3 SDK 3.8.1 headers. Their use by
src/lib.rs, src/plugin.rs, src/gui.rs and src/host.rs implements those interfaces directly in Rust.
tests/abi-smoke.cpp uses the official headers for independent ABI validation.

GUI dependencies and fonts

The GUI uses egui 0.37.0, egui_glow 0.37.0 and baseview 0.4.1 with
OpenGL rendering. Cargo.lock records the exact dependency versions.
The bundle's Contents/Resources/Licenses/dependencies/ contains the target's
crate and bundled-font license notices. Its index.json lists the versions,
license expressions and public sources. This includes the egui fonts and
their MIT, SIL Open Font License and Ubuntu Font Licence notices.
For crates whose published archive omits license text, licenses/upstream/
contains the original notice and its public source.
